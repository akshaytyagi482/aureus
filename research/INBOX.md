# Documents needed from India

These government sites block or time out on connections from outside India. Download each file
from India and upload it to `research/inbox/` (on GitHub: open the folder on branch
`claude/zealous-gauss-5wpqrv` → *Add file → Upload files*), or attach it in the chat.
Keep the original file name. Tick the box when done.

## Priority 1: states shown as unverified

- [ ] **Uttarakhand.** MSME Policy 2023 (notification 1145/VII-3-23/04(01)-MSME/2023) and its implementation guidelines.
  https://destinationuttarakhand.uk.gov.in/swcsincentive/common/protected/msme_policy/MSMEpolicy.pdf
  Also the **district category A/B/C/D annexure**.
- [ ] **Madhya Pradesh.** MP MSME Development Policy 2025 (notification F 0057/2025/1/65, 22-02-2025).
  Try https://mpmsme.gov.in → Policies, or ask the local DIC.
- [ ] **Andhra Pradesh.** G.O.Ms.No.69 dated 26-10-2024 (MSME & EDP 4.0), plus the amendments G.O.Ms.No.22 (17-02-2025) and G.O.Ms.No.28 (2025).
  https://apmsmeone.ap.gov.in → Existing Policy
- [ ] **West Bengal.** The official notification of **WBIS 2026 for MSMEs** (in force from 01-10-2026), including its zone A/B/C list.
  https://myenterprisewb.in or https://wbmsme.gov.in
- [ ] **Jammu & Kashmir.** Industrial Policy 2021-30 (GO 117-IND dated 19-04-2021), with its zone A/B list.
  https://industriescommerce.jk.gov.in/Schemes%20and%20Policies.html
- [ ] **Tripura.** TIIPIS 2022 notification and the TIIP 2024 notification (07-10-2024).
  https://industries.tripura.gov.in/state-scheme-details
- [ ] **Mizoram.** Industrial and Investment Policy of Mizoram 2025 (Gazette Extraordinary No. 597, 22-08-2025).
  https://industries.mizoram.gov.in

## Priority 2: zone lists (needed before estimates work there)

- [ ] **Gujarat.** The MSME scheme GR under Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026 (reported GR dated 25-09-2026), and the **taluka Category A/B list** (Annexure-A of the Oct 2026 classification GR).
  https://ic.gujarat.gov.in (Schemes / GR section)
- [ ] **Uttar Pradesh.** The official list of districts in Bundelkhand / Purvanchal / Madhyanchal / Paschimanchal used by the MSME Policy 2022.
  https://msme1connect.up.gov.in
